# Ambient Field — 8 Scene Arrangement

Drone center: **D Lydian** (D – E – F# – G# – A – B – C#)
- **D** = grounding root
- **F#** = "Jupiter" tone (expansion/abundance, ~183.58 Hz cosmic octave)
- **C#** = "Om" tone (~136.1 Hz cosmic octave)
- Triad anchor for cross-zone safety: **D – A – F#**

## Order (moves by fifths, each scene shares a pivot pitch with the next)

D → A → E → B → F# → C# → G# → D (finale, loops back to Scene 1)

| # | Root | Vibe | 4-5 stem roles | Pivot to next |
|---|------|------|------------------|---------------|
| 1 | **D** | Grounding, opening | low drone (D), 5th pad (A), soft texture, sparse melodic motif | shared **A** |
| 2 | **A** | Lift-off | drone (A), 3rd up (C#/Om), rhythmic pulse/perc, airy pad | shared **E** |
| 3 | **E** | Open, breathing | drone (E), 5th (B), field recording/foley, melodic fragment | shared **B** |
| 4 | **B** | Tension building | drone (B), F# pad (Jupiter), textural noise/shimmer, motif | shared **F#** |
| 5 | **F#** | Expansion, "Jupiter" peak | drone (F#), C# pad, brighter lead/melody, rhythmic layer | shared **C#** |
| 6 | **C#** | Cosmic/"Om" core | drone (C#), G# pad, whispered prayers layer, sparse texture | shared **G#** |
| 7 | **G#** | Outer edge, most "open" | drone (G#), D pad (tension back to start), foley, motif | shared **D** |
| 8 | **D — Finale** | Return/collapse | recap: D drone + A + F# + C# all together, full prayer/voice layer | loops to Scene 1 |

## How they combine

- Each scene's **drone + pad** (first 2 stems) are the "always safe" pair — within D Lydian, so they layer cleanly under any other scene's extra stems even when zones bleed together spatially.
- The **3rd–5th stems** (motif/texture/foley) are the "personality" layers — these change scene to scene and can be triggered solo or stacked.
- **Transitions:** fade out the outgoing scene's personality stems first, let the shared pivot drone/pad bridge for a few bars, then bring in the new scene's personality stems.
- **Scene 8 (Finale)** is the "everything" moment — designed to absorb stems from Scenes 1 (D), 5 (F#), and 6 (C#) for a big converging finish, pairing well with the whispered-prayers layering idea.
